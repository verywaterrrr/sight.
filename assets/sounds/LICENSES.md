# Sample credits

Piano: Salamander Grand Piano, Yamaha C5 recordings by Alexander Holm. CC BY3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/. Source: https://github.com/Tonejs/audio/tree/master/salamander. The app uses twelve samples, converted to mono MP3 and trimmed to ten seconds.

Female and male choir: Sonatina Symphonic Orchestra by Mattias Westlund, looped edition by Signal Experiments and Peter Eastman. Creative Commons Sampling Plus1.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/sampling+/1.0/. Source and attribution: https://github.com/peastman/sso. Seven samples are cropped to their published sustain loops and converted to mono WAV. This app and these transformed samples are distributed for noncommercial choir practice. Original sample attribution is in the upstream README: https://github.com/peastman/sso#license.

The choir plays sustained vowel sounds, without singing printed lyrics. Each recording is pitched to the written note; female recordings serve soprano/alto, male recordings serve tenor/bass. Piano notes use real grand-piano recordings.
